# Settings

## Me

Your name and colour.

**First name** and **Last name** are used to fill in your name on templates such as Check in, so you don't have to type it each time. You can still change it on any message, and that only affects that message. Tick **My legal name is different** if the name on your documents isn't your first name and last name, then type your **Full legal name**. Leave them all blank and the name gaps start empty.

Your names stay on this device.

Tick **Use my first name in chats** to use your first name for your side of a [Side by side](side-by-side.md) chat. Your chat name box is hidden while it's on. To use a different name, tap **Use a different name** in a chat's names and colours, which turns this off. Otherwise, set a name and colour of your own for your side of a chat.

## Appearance

Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System**. System matches your device's light or dark setting.

## Show mode colours

The colours a message starts in when you [show it](show.md): black on white, white on black, or yellow on black.

## Built-in templates

**Restore built-in templates** brings back any built-in templates you've deleted. If you've edited any built-in templates, it asks whether to restore those too: **Restore all** replaces your changes with the version that came with Deaftly, and **Keep my changes** leaves them as they are.

# Listen for me

Listen for me is for when someone wants to talk to you. Deaftly turns what they say into text on your phone, so you can read along. The audio isn't kept.

## Using it

1. On the home screen, tap **Listen for me**.
2. The first time, Android asks to use the microphone. Tap **Allow microphone**. If it's blocked, the screen offers to open settings.
3. Show the other person the message at the top: "I'm deaf. Please speak to my phone and I'll read what you say."
4. Their words appear as they speak. A pause starts a new line.

## The message for the other person

The message at the top starts upside down, so it faces someone opposite you. Tap the turn button to turn it the right way up, for when you hold the phone up to show them. Deaftly remembers which way you chose.

## Pausing

Tap the microphone button at the top to pause listening, and tap it again to listen again. Deaftly also stops listening when you leave the screen or switch to another app.

If voice typing isn't available, the microphone button turns grey and the screen says why. You can still tap **Continue side by side** and type.

## When you want to reply

Tap **Continue side by side** and pass them the phone. Everything they said comes with you as their messages, it stays their turn, and voice typing stays on. See [Side by side](side-by-side.md).

## Ending

Tap **End listening**. If they've said anything, you're asked whether to end. Ending deletes what they said.

The text size follows the chat text size you chose in Side by side.

# Side by side

Side by side is for a back-and-forth conversation on one phone. You pass the phone between you and take turns to type. Each person's messages appear in their own colour, like a script, so it's easy to follow who said what.

## Taking turns

- The switch shows whose turn it is. Flip it to change turns.
- When it's the other person's turn, a prompt tells them where to type: "Your turn: type your reply here".
- Tap **Send** to add a message.

## Names, colours and sides

- **Names and colours** lets you set a name and colour for each of you. If you leave a name blank, it shows as "Me" or "Them". If you use your first name in chats, your side shows it with **Use a different name**, which turns that off so you can type another.
- **Switch sides** swaps which side of the screen each of you is on, so it matches where you're standing.

## Writing messages

- **Bold**, **Italic** and **Highlight** add emphasis.
- **Insert template** adds one of your [templates](templates.md).
- **Change text size** switches between small, medium and large.

## Voice typing

Voice typing is for the other person, so the microphone button only shows on their turn. It turns what they say into text on this device. The audio isn't kept.

- Tap the microphone button next to **Send** to start. The first time, Android asks to use the microphone. If it's blocked, the button offers to open settings.
- While it's on, their words appear in the chat as they speak. A pause sends the message, so each message stays short.
- Tap the button again to stop. Anything already shown is sent first.
- It only listens on the other person's turn, while Deaftly is open. When you switch to your turn, you get the keyboard back.
- If voice typing isn't available, the microphone button turns grey with a line through it. Tap it to see why. You can still type as usual.

To fix a mistake in a voice-typed message, they tap it on their turn. Voice typing stops while they edit, and the message stays in the chat. See Copying, quoting, editing and deleting below.

## Copying, quoting, editing and deleting

Long-press a message to **Edit**, **Copy**, **Quote** or **Delete** it. A quoted message appears above your reply.

You can edit your own messages, on your own turn. Tap the message, or long-press it and choose **Edit**. It stays where it is in the chat, and its text, formatting and quote go into the text box under "Editing message". Make your changes and tap **Send** to replace it. It then shows EDITED under it. Tap the X beside "Editing message" to cancel and leave the message as it was.

Whatever you had already written in the box is set aside while you edit, and comes back when you send or cancel. The other person can do the same with their own messages on their turn, including voice-typed ones. You can't edit each other's messages. If you tap the other person's message, a note reminds you.

## Ending the chat

Tap **End chat**. If the chat has messages, you're asked whether to keep it:

- **Keep** saves the chat so you can read it or carry on later.
- **Delete** ends the chat and deletes the messages.
- **Keep chatting** goes back to the chat.

## Saved chats

Chats you keep appear in **Saved chats** on the home screen. Tap one to open it and carry on where you left off. Changes are saved as you go, and ending a kept chat saves it without asking.

To delete a saved chat, tap the bin next to it. If a saved chat can't be opened, it shows as "This chat cannot be read", and you can still delete it. Kept chats stay on this phone and, if you've turned it on, in your Android backup.
